Field Manual · Gear & Loadouts

The Forge

Division Notice: All builds are assuming you are HR100+

If you've made it to this page, we're assuming you're minmaxing. A base-common loadout is already plenty for a generalist, progression-focused build, so what follows is the final, endgame kit for each of the four playstyles, gear and decorations included, with the reasoning behind every skill choice. It should be known that if you discover any builds of your own that are better, please feel free to make a post over at the Hunter Forum.

Dash Dancing

Weapon: Calamitous Angel (Gogmazios Artian + any element)

Equipment

Charm

Decorations

Why This Build

This is your bread 'n butter build. Nothing fancy — the Bow doesn't need Divine Blessing or the ordinary defensive skills, but instead it leans on Refresh and Physique. Those two skills give you the stamina to comfortably spam your Charging Sidestep and Discerning Dodge. Critical Eye, Element Attack, Charge Master, Tetrad Shot, Spread/Power Shots, Peak Performance, Weakness Exploit, and Burst are your primary damage skills — they'll always be up if you're doing it right.

Dragonpiercer Sniping

Equipment

Charm

Decorations

Why This Build

If you look closely, the armor and weapon are basically the same as Dash Dancing — the difference lies in the decorations and the charm. This build runs one Refresh jewel, so stamina won't come back as fast from neutral, but it packs two Earplug jewels, letting you shrug off roars and focus on your own positioning (most monsters in Wilds, pre-Ascendance at least, have weak roars anyway). Keep in mind this is an element-focused Dragonpiercer build with Burst attached, so your rotation should look like: Charging Sidestep, Power Shot, Dragonpiercer. That keeps Burst at 100% uptime.

8-Gauge Bow

Equipment

Charm

Decorations

Why This Build

The usual suspects when it comes to armor, but the charm is much more flexible — you mainly just want Divine Blessing, or another skill that adds to your damage (Tetrad Shot, Critical, Burst; it's flexible). This build sacrifices some Peak Performance for more Stamina Surge and Earplugs, because the whole thing is built around shenanigans and staying mobile. If you get slowed, or your flow gets ruined by a roar or by running out of stamina, you're basically done for.

Hybrid

Weapon: Calamitous Angel (Gogmazios Artian + any element)

Equipment

Charm

Decorations

Why This Build

This build drops Earplugs entirely, but runs both Piercing Shots and Spread/Power Shots — meaning you can, technically, play into both Dragonpiercer and Dash Dancing thanks to Stamina Surge and Constitution. But again: no Earplugs, no divine protection, nothing. You have to hit your Discerning Dodges or you're not making it anywhere. The whole point of this build is feeling good — dodging everything and using your entire kit to blow up a monster like you're the main character.

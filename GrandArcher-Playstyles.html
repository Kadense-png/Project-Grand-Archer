Bow Playstyles

Four Ways to Loose an Arrow

(Or just the most popular and funny ways)

The Bow is the most (self-proclaimed) flexible and stylish weapon in Monster Hunter Wilds. It comes with an infinite amount of playstyles, but we recognized four of the most effective, popular, and stylish, those being: Dash Dancing, Dragonpiercer Sniping, 8 Gauge Bow, and Hybrid.

Playstyle 01

Dash Dancing

★★★☆☆

Range: Short–Mid Focus: Repositioning

Dash dancing takes advantage of the bow's unique dodge. Used after shooting in focus mode it becomes a Charging Sidestep (CS). Timed perfectly against an incoming attack it becomes a Discerning Dodge (DD) — restoring stamina and granting two charge levels instantly. Near 100% uptime; any monster becomes a pincushion while you remain untouchable.

Charging Side-Step Discerning Dodge Stamina Restoration ~100% uptime

Playstyle 02

Dragonpiercer Sniping

★★★★★

Range: Long Focus: Positioning, Monster knowledge

Built around the Dragonpiercer (DP) — a unique piercing move that also deals slicing damage, enabling tail severing. Its effective range far exceeds the bow's normal Critical Range, enabling true long-range sniping. Been through a lot of nerfs, it used to be the best way to play bow meta-wise, but with modern builds and proper skill investment it keeps pace with Dash Dancing, I think.

Dragonpiercer(DP) Tail severing Patience

Playstyle 03

8-Gauge Bow

★★★★★★★★

Range: Short Focus: Luck, Fun, Silly

The one off-meta entry — and it fully owns it. Built around hugging the monster and taking FULL advantage of Close-range coatings and DD to deal massive point-blank damage chunks using Thousand Dragons and Power Shots, which are all Spread attacks.

Thousand Dragons Power Shot Close-range Coating

Playstyle 04

Hybrid

★★★★★

Range: All-range Focus: Adaptive

Everything in one. A jack of all trades — but remember the full saying: more oftentimes better than a master of one. Sacrifice some comfort skills for damage, rely on your own read of DD to consistently dodge attacks, and use everything in the bow's arsenal. The most fun and skill-intensive playstyle bow has to offer.

Versatile High Skill floor No health
